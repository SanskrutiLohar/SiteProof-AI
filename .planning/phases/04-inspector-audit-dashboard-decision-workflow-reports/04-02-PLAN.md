# Plan 04-02: Inspection Scheduler, Immutable Audit Trail & Print-Ready Reports

## Objective
Build the Physical Site Inspection Scheduler, the immutable audit activity timeline, and the print-ready Verified Audit Report Certificate generator with regulatory authority letterhead, QR verification code, and 5-stage quantity reconciliation summary.

## Requirements Covered
- **AUDIT-04**: Physical Site Inspection Scheduler with assigned auditor, date/time booking, and checklist tracking.
- **AUDIT-05**: Printable Verified Milestone Audit Report with authority letterhead, QR code, 5-stage reconciliation summary, and immutable activity timeline.

---

## Technical Scope

### 1. Inspection Scheduler Modal & Management (`src/components/audit/InspectionSchedulerModal.tsx`)
- Booking form:
  - Inspection Date & Time.
  - Assigned Lead Inspector (select from registered auditors).
  - Meeting Point / Site Sector / Gate Entry (e.g., "Pier 12 - Site Gate B").
  - On-site PPE & Testing Checklist (e.g., Hard Hat & High-Vis, Rebound Hammer Test Kit, Slump Cone, Concrete Core Sampling, Measurement Tape).
  - Inspection Objectives & Special Instructions.
- Updates `schedules` in `ProjectContext` and logs activity.
- Interactive status updater (`Mark Completed` with field remarks).

### 2. Immutable Audit Activity Timeline (`src/components/audit/AuditActivityTimeline.tsx`)
- Visual chronological log of every milestone activity:
  - Milestone creation & BOQ baseline definition.
  - Contractor evidence submissions (photos, challans, invoices).
  - AI Gemini Multimodal Vision & Document OCR execution.
  - Discrepancy flag generation and inspector resolution.
  - Physical inspection scheduling & completion.
  - Inspector certified sign-off and milestone status changes.
- Timestamp, actor avatar, role badge, and cryptographic audit hash badge.

### 3. Print-Ready Verified Audit Report Modal (`src/components/audit/AuditReportModal.tsx`)
- Official, printable certificate view formatted for regulatory audit and PDF export:
  - Authority Header: *National Infrastructure Verification & Safety Board*.
  - Project Title, Project Code, Client Name, and Contractor EPC.
  - Milestone Scope, Status, and Weight Percentage.
  - 5-Stage Contractual Quantity Reconciliation Table (Ordered vs Delivered vs Billed vs Paid vs Consumed).
  - Key Photo & Document Evidence Catalog with GPS coordinates and timestamps.
  - Digital Inspector Stamp with Certified Sign-off Timestamp.
  - Cryptographic Verification Hash & QR Code.
  - Native browser `window.print()` trigger with print-optimized CSS rules.

### 4. Context & State Engine Enhancement (`src/context/ProjectContext.tsx`)
- Add `schedules: InspectionSchedule[]` and `auditLogs: AuditLogEntry[]` states with `localStorage` persistence.
- Add helper methods:
  - `scheduleInspection: (data: Omit<InspectionSchedule, 'id'>) => InspectionSchedule`
  - `updateInspectionStatus: (id: string, status: InspectionSchedule['status']) => void`
  - `getMilestoneSchedules: (milestoneId: string) => InspectionSchedule[]`
  - `addAuditLog: (entry: Omit<AuditLogEntry, 'id' | 'timestamp'>) => void`
  - `getMilestoneAuditLogs: (milestoneId: string) => AuditLogEntry[]`
  - `recordMilestoneDecision: (decision: MilestoneDecision) => void`
