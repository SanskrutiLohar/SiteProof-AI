# Plan 03-02: 5-Stage Quantity Reconciliation & Discrepancy Matrix

## Objective
Implement the 5-stage contractual quantity reconciliation engine and automated discrepancy anomaly detection system, updating the project state and rendering visual audit insights in the contractor and inspector views.

## Requirements Covered
- **AI-03**: 5-stage quantity reconciliation (Ordered vs Delivered vs Billed vs Paid vs Consumed).
- **AI-04**: Anomaly & Discrepancy Engine (detects duplicate documents, date anomalies, and quantity variances).

---

## Technical Scope

### 1. Reconciliation & Discrepancy Engine (`src/lib/reconciliationEngine.ts`)
- `reconcileMilestoneQuantities(milestoneId, boqItems, evidenceList)`:
  - Aggregates quantities from contractual BOQ (`Ordered`), Delivery Challans (`Delivered`), Tax Invoices (`Billed`), Certified Claims (`Paid`), and Vision AI estimates (`Consumed`).
  - Computes variances: `Delivered - Ordered`, `Billed - Delivered`, `Consumed - Billed`.
  - Generates `DiscrepancyFlag[]` records with severity levels (`low`, `medium`, `high`, `critical`):
    - **Over-Billing**: Billed quantity exceeds delivered quantity.
    - **Under-Delivery**: Delivered quantity is less than contracted BOQ milestone requirement.
    - **Duplicate Invoices**: Identical invoice/challan number found across records.
    - **Date Anomaly**: Invoice timestamp occurs prior to material dispatch ticket.
    - **Physical Lag**: Vision AI progress estimate is significantly lower than claimed billing percentage.

### 2. Discrepancy Flag State & Context Engine (`src/context/ProjectContext.tsx`)
- Add `discrepancies: DiscrepancyFlag[]` state with `localStorage` persistence.
- Add methods:
  - `runMilestoneAIAnalysis: (milestoneId: string) => Promise<void>`
  - `getMilestoneDiscrepancies: (milestoneId: string) => DiscrepancyFlag[]`
  - `getProjectDiscrepancies: (projectId: string) => DiscrepancyFlag[]`
  - `resolveDiscrepancy: (flagId: string, resolutionNote: string) => void`

### 3. AI Verification Cockpit Banner & Discrepancy Alert Drawer (`src/components/ai/AIDiscrepancyPanel.tsx`)
- Displays real-time AI audit status:
  - Overall AI Verification Confidence score gauge (e.g. 92%).
  - 5-stage Reconciliation Visual Summary Card.
  - Active Discrepancy Flags list with severity badges, root cause explanation, and inspector resolution action.
  - "Run Full AI Verification Audit" one-click button.

### 4. Lightbox AI Insights Integration (`src/components/evidence/EvidenceLightbox.tsx`)
- Expand Lightbox side-drawer to display parsed Gemini Vision elements or OCR extracted invoice tables when an item has been analyzed.

### 5. Page Level Integration
- Integrate `AIDiscrepancyPanel` into `ContractorMilestonePage.tsx` and `ProjectDetailsPage.tsx`.
